# Mobile Sales Performance Dashboard 📱

An end-to-end Power BI project designed to analyze and visualize mobile phone sales data. This dashboard provides actionable insights into sales trends, customer behavior, and brand performance to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

##  Key Features
* *KPI Tracking:* Real-time visibility of Total Sales ($769M), Units Sold (19K), Total Customers (988), and Average Ratings.
* *Sales Trends:* Monthly performance tracking to identify seasonal peaks and dips.
* *Geographic Analysis:* Sales distribution across major Indian cities like Delhi, Mumbai, and Ranchi.
* *Brand & Model Insights:* Breakdown of market share by top brands (Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, etc.) and specific mobile models.
* *Demographic Segmentation:* Analyzing customer age groups to understand the target audience.
* *Payment Method Analysis:* Visualizing the preference for UPI, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, and Cash.
* *Interactive Slicers:* Dynamic filtering by City, Brand, Payment Method, and Month.

##  Tools & Technologies
* *Power BI Desktop:* For data modeling and visualization.
* *DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):* For calculated measures and complex KPIs.
* *Power Query:* For data cleaning and transformation (ETL).
* *UI/UX:* Designed with both Light and Dark mode themes for better accessibility.

##  Key Insights from the Data
1.  *Top Performer:* Apple leads the market share with *21.01%* of total sales.
2.  *Top City:* *Delhi* is the highest-grossing city with sales exceeding *200M*.
3.  *Customer Profile:* The *30-40 age group* represents the largest customer segment.
4.  *Payment Trends:* *UPI* is the most preferred payment method at *26.22%*, closely followed by Debit Cards.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the .pbix file in Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the slicers on the right to interact with the data.
